The Technology

Sensor technology that captures what the eye misses

Drive Hockey's PT100 sensor straps to a player's shoulder pads and captures over 3,000 data points per second — every edge, acceleration, turn and force event across the entire session. No cameras. No GPS. Pure biomechanical data.

Multi-patented Canadian technology covered by Sports Illustrated and backed by former NHL scouting leadership. Ice Cold Analytics is the only authorized Drive Hockey distributor in Alberta — the exclusive gateway to this technology through a structured evaluation program.

What Gets Measured

Five skating metrics. All in context.

Scores are normalized on a percentile scale of 25–100 within each age group — results always mean something relative to peers, not just raw numbers.

⚡

Top Speed

Maximum velocity during full-flight skating events — how fast your athlete actually skates.

🚀

Acceleration

First-step burst from stationary, moving and with puck — distance gained in 1.5 and 3.0 seconds.

🔄

Agility

Edge quality, turn speed and transition skating through angular events during play.

💪

Conditioning

Force capacity — how well peak output is maintained as a session progresses.

🛑

Deceleration

Edge braking and stopping power — critical for positioning and defensive reads.
