The Technology

NHL-level analytics. Amateur hockey budgets.

Drive Hockey's PT100 sensor straps to a player's shoulder pads and captures over 3,000 data points per second — every edge, acceleration, force event and shift across the entire session. The same multi-patented technology used at the pro level, now available to Alberta athletes through Ice Cold Analytics.

Ice Cold Analytics is an authorized Drive Hockey distributor in Alberta — the exclusive gateway to this technology through a structured Player Development & Analytics Combine program built for minor hockey.

What Gets Measured

Fitness. Skill. Game Impact. Benchmark Rankings.

Drive Hockey automatically breaks down over 25 skating abilities and performance metrics. Every score is benchmarked against peers in the same age group — so results always mean something in context.

💪

Fitness

Strength, conditioning and endurance measured through force output, force capacity and active load metrics across every shift.

⛸️

Skill

Auto breakdown of 20+ skating abilities — top speed, acceleration, agility, deceleration and more, captured without cameras or GPS.

🏒

Game Impact

How individual players contribute when on ice — skating events, angular speed, linear skating, backward speed and transition data.

📊

Benchmark Rankings

Leaderboards and custom weighted rankings against group benchmarks — athletes see exactly where they stand relative to peers.

📈

Progress Over Time

Track each athlete's development across events. Measurable goals, growth trends and improvement tracked event to event.
