Blended intelligence, not just numbers

Skating data alone can't tell the whole story. ICA's composite score weights observational evaluation at 60% — capturing compete level, hockey sense, skills, and character — then blends in Drive Hockey's objective skating metrics at 40%.

All Drive Hockey metrics are normalized on a percentile scale of 25–100 within each age group, so your athlete's score means something in the context of their peers — not just an abstract number.

  • Consistent evaluators ensure comparability across events
  • Percentile normalization gives age-appropriate context
  • Repeated events build a true development trajectory
  • Separate goalie methodology for position-specific accuracy
