What We Stand For

Two ideas, and we build everything around them.

01

Pro-level measurement
should be accessible.

The technology that evaluates junior and pro prospects has no business stopping at the elite level. As the authorized Drive Hockey distributor for Alberta, our job is to get it onto community ice — with no equipment for your association to buy, no software licence to carry, and no technical staff to hire.

02

Measurement is what
drives real results.

Opinions don't compound. Numbers do. When every skater is measured the same way on the same ice, coaches can coach to actual gaps, athletes train with a target, and you can show a parent or a board precisely what changed between September and March.

Why Directors Call Us

The four problems you already know about.

Every director we speak with raises the same issues. Here is how objective data addresses each one.

"I can't prove our program actually develops players."
Measure every athlete at intake, then again through the season. Development stops being a claim in your brochure and becomes a number you can put in front of a parent, a board, or a sponsor.
"Nothing separates us from the program across town."
Ice Cold Analytics is the authorized Drive Hockey distributor for Alberta. The programs that move first hold a capability their competitors cannot buy elsewhere in the province.
"My coaches won't adopt another piece of software."
They don't have to. We bring the equipment, run the session, and deliver finished reports. No installation, no training day, no new system for your staff to learn.
"Parents question every evaluation decision we make."
Hand them a report instead of an explanation. When speed, acceleration and conditioning are measured the same way for every skater, the conversation changes from opinion to evidence.
How It Works

Sensors on your ice. Reports in minutes.

Accessible means accessible. Your association buys no hardware and your coaches learn no software.

1

We set up

Location anchors go on the outside of the glass and sensors strap to shoulder pads. About 15 minutes, and no arena internet required.

2

Your coaches run the ice

Nothing changes about your session plan. There is no equipment for your staff to operate and no system for them to learn.

3

Reports come back

Over 20 skating metrics per athlete, benchmarked by age group, ready within minutes for you, your coaches and your families.
