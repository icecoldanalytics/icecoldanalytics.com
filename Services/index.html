The Process

How a Combine Works

Every Ice Cold Analytics event follows the same proven process — from on-ice drills to a report that lands in your inbox.

01

Register

Sign up your athlete online. Spots are limited per age group to ensure quality data collection and individual attention.

02

On-Ice Testing

Athletes skate through standardized drills with Drive Hockey AI sensors — placed around the rink and worn on shoulder pads — capturing over 20 individual skills in real time.

03

Expert Evaluation

Certified coaches observe each athlete and provide qualitative insight alongside the sensor data for a complete picture.

04

Development Report

Your athlete receives a professional report with their analytics, expert notes, and specific areas to target in training.
