The Smart Arena

Any Rink.
Anywhere.

The Drive Hockey Smart Arena system transforms any Alberta rink into a professional-grade analytics environment — no permanent installation required.

What Is the Smart Arena

A Professional Analytics
Environment, Anywhere

The Smart Arena is Drive Hockey's portable sensor setup — a complete AI-powered analytics system that can be deployed at any rink in under 15 minutes. Sensors mount around the boards. Athletes wear a lightweight tag on their shoulder pads. The AI starts capturing data immediately.

For community hockey in Alberta, this is a game-changer. Elite programs have permanent analytics infrastructure. With the Smart Arena, your arena in Beaumont or Sherwood Park or Edmonton is just as capable as any pro facility for a combine or evaluation day.

System Features

Built for Any Rink

⚡

15-Minute Setup

The full system is operational in under 15 minutes. No permanent installation, no rink modifications, no advance booking of specialty equipment.

📍

Fully Portable

Everything travels in compact cases. The Smart Arena can be deployed at any rink in Alberta — community arenas, practice facilities, or multi-rink complexes.

🤖

AI-Powered

The same Drive Hockey AI platform used at NHL combines. Sensors track every player in real time and automatically identify and measure 20+ individual skills.

👕

Shoulder-Pad Sensor

Athletes wear a single lightweight sensor on their shoulder pads. No blade modifications, no wrist units, nothing that interferes with normal skating.

📊

Real-Time Data

Data is captured and processed in real time during testing sessions, so results can be reviewed almost immediately after athletes leave the ice.

📄

Professional Reports

Every athlete receives a professional development report with their data benchmarked against Drive Hockey's Player Performance Index database.

Who Uses It

Designed for Every Level

Athletes & Parents

Individual Combines

Individual athletes register for a combine event. They skate through standardized testing drills and receive their personal analytics report — an honest, data-driven snapshot of where they stand and what to work on.

Teams & Associations

Team Evaluation Days

Bring the Smart Arena to your rink for a full-team evaluation. Every player gets individual analytics, and coaching staff receive aggregate team data to inform their development planning.

Academies & Leagues

Seasonal Programming

Use the Smart Arena for pre-season baselines and post-season comparison. Track player development over time with consistent, objective data that removes the guesswork from evaluations.
