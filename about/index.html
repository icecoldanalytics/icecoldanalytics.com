The Founder

From the Ice at U of A
to the Data Room

I'm Stacey Phillips — a former University of Alberta hockey player who spent years competing and always wondered the same thing: what if I actually knew what to fix?

Coaches give feedback. Trainers give drills. But the data? The kind of precise, measurable insights that tell you exactly where your edges fail, where your stride loses power, where you're leaving time on the ice — that was never available to us as players. I had to guess, and so did my coaches.

I founded Ice Cold Analytics because I know firsthand what it feels like to work hard without the right information. As an authorized Drive Hockey distributor, I bring the same sensor technology used at the NHL combine level to minor hockey athletes across Alberta — at a price point that works for regular families.

This is the service I wish had existed when I was developing as a player.

Our Mission

Democratizing High-Performance Analytics

High-performance analytics used to be reserved for elite programs and professional scouts. We believe that's wrong. A kid playing AA hockey in Edmonton deserves to know just as much about their skating mechanics as a prospect at a national combine.

Ice Cold Analytics exists to close that gap — using real sensor data, expert evaluation, and professional development reports to give every athlete and their family the knowledge they need to improve, with purpose.

The Technology

Powered by Drive Hockey

Every Ice Cold Analytics combine runs on the Drive Hockey sensor and AI platform — the same technology trusted by professional scouts and NHL-level development programs. The portable system sets up in 15 minutes with sensors around the rink and a lightweight sensor worn on each player's shoulder pads.

  • ✓AI automatically identifies and measures over 20 individual on-ice skills
  • ✓Captures skating mechanics, acceleration patterns, and skill performance breakdowns
  • ✓Tracks crossover, weaving, and tight turn profiles — including entry, mid, and exit speeds
  • ✓Measures tactical positioning, hockey IQ, and decision-making
  • ✓Player Performance Index (PPI) benchmarks athletes against a database of all skill levels
  • ✓Data paired with expert evaluation and delivered as a professional development report
