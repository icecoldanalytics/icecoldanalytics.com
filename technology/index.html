What Gets Measured

The Same Standard
For Every Skater

Every athlete in your program is measured the same way, on the same ice, under the same conditions. That consistency is what makes the results defensible when a coach, a parent or a board asks how a decision was reached.

  • ✓Top speed — peak velocity reached across the session
  • ✓Acceleration — first-step quickness and burst off the line
  • ✓Agility — tight turns, crossovers and direction changes
  • ✓Deceleration — stopping power and edge control
  • ✓Conditioning — work rate and output across extended shifts
  • ✓Force output — power generated through the skating stride
  • ✓Benchmarks — every score ranked against peers in the same age group

Conditioning Analytics

Fitness Meets
Ice Intelligence

Beyond skating skills, Drive Hockey captures conditioning data that shows how an athlete's performance changes as fatigue sets in — a dimension of player development that's nearly impossible to evaluate with the naked eye.

Understanding how an athlete's mechanics degrade under fatigue is one of the most valuable insights a coach or trainer can have. The platform makes it measurable.

The Smart Arena

A Portable Rink System
That Comes To You

Nothing gets installed and nothing gets left behind. The Smart Arena travels to your rink, runs on its own network, and packs up when the session ends. Once we calibrate your rink, it stays calibrated for every future visit.

  • Arena Anchors

    Small location-sensing anchors attach to the outside of the rink glass — never on the ice surface and never in the way of your session. They map the full playing surface so every stride is placed in context.

  • Player Sensors

    Lightweight sensors strap to shoulder pads before players step on the ice. No blades, no wires, nothing an athlete will notice, and each one is pre-assigned to a player profile before the session starts.

  • Self-Contained Network

    The system creates its own Wi-Fi network, so it works in older community rinks with no usable internet. It runs all day on rechargeable lithium batteries.

  • Processing & Delivery

    When the session ends we sync the data and AI processes every event. Finished reports are available within minutes to you, your coaching staff and the families you choose to share them with.
