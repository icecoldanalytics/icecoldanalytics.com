Drive Hockey is the AI-powered sensor platform used by professional scouts and development programs at the highest levels of the game. Ice Cold Analytics brings that same technology to minor hockey athletes across Alberta as an authorized distributor.

The system sets up in 15 minutes at any rink. Sensors are positioned around the boards and athletes wear a lightweight sensor on their shoulder pads. From there, the AI does the work — automatically identifying and measuring skills as athletes skate through standardized testing drills.

No guesswork. No subjectivity. Just data.

What Gets Measured

Precision Across
Every Skill Set

The Drive Hockey AI doesn't just track where athletes are on the ice — it understands what they're doing and how well they're doing it. Every rep, every edge, every turn is captured and categorized.

  • ✓Skating mechanics — stride efficiency, power, and edge control
  • ✓Acceleration patterns — first step quickness and top-speed development
  • ✓Crossover profiles — entry speed, radius, mid-turn, and exit velocity
  • ✓Tight turn mechanics — direction changes and agility patterns
  • ✓Hockey IQ — tactical positioning and decision-making on-ice
  • ✓Conditioning metrics — work rate and recovery patterns
  • ✓Force output — power generation through the skating stride

Conditioning Analytics

Fitness Meets
Ice Intelligence

Beyond skating skills, Drive Hockey captures conditioning data that shows how an athlete's performance changes as fatigue sets in — a dimension of player development that's nearly impossible to evaluate with the naked eye.

Understanding how an athlete's mechanics degrade under fatigue is one of the most valuable insights a coach or trainer can have. The platform makes it measurable.

The System

How It All Works

01

Setup

AI sensors are positioned around the rink boards. The system is fully portable and operational in 15 minutes at any arena.

02

Player Tag

Each athlete wears a lightweight sensor on their shoulder pads. No blades, no wires — completely unobtrusive.

03

AI Capture

As athletes skate through standardized drills, the AI automatically identifies and measures every skill in real time.

04

PPI Report

Results are benchmarked via the Player Performance Index and delivered as a professional development report.
