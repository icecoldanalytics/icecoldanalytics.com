The Process

How We Work With Your Program

From first conversation to reports in your inbox. We fit around your existing calendar rather than asking you to build a new event.

01

Conversation

We talk through your program, your age groups and where in your calendar the data would be most useful — tryouts, evaluation camps or in-season.

02

We Come To You

We bring the Smart Arena to your rink and set up in about 15 minutes. Your association buys no equipment and your staff operates nothing.

03

Your Session Runs

Your coaches run the ice exactly as planned. Sensors capture over 20 skating metrics for every athlete while we stay out of the way.

04

Reports Come Back

One development report per athlete plus a director summary across the group, benchmarked by age and ready within minutes.

What You and Your Families Receive

📈

Skating Analytics

Over 20 skating metrics measured automatically — top speed, acceleration, agility, deceleration, conditioning and force output — with every score benchmarked against peers in the same age group.

📋

Director Summary

A view across the whole group, not just one athlete at a time — rankings by metric, age-group distribution, and the gaps worth building your development plan around.

🎯

Development Targets

Specific, actionable areas for each athlete to focus on — not generic advice, but targets tied directly to what the measurement showed.

📄

Professional Report

A clean single-page document per athlete, ready to hand to families or keep as the baseline you measure the next session against.
